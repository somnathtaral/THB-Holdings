Division 01 – Founder Advisory

I've built it, broken it, scaled it & sold it. Now I help you do it.

Interim and fractional support for FMCG and consumer brand founders, sub-£5m revenue. Commercial, financial, and operational expertise from someone who's actually lived the journey.

Who I work with

For founders who've raised the money. Now they have to make it work.

The £500k–£5m revenue band is where most consumer brands either find their stride or quietly run out of road. The gap between "promising brand" and "real business" is paved with decisions founders are rarely equipped to make alone.

i.

You've landed your first big retailer

Selfridges, Whole Foods, Holland & Barrett – you got the listing. Now you need to keep it, expand it, and not get crushed by margin compression and slotting fees.

ii.

Cash is tight but ambition is bigger

You've raised an angel round, perhaps a seed. The runway is real but finite. Working capital, gross margin, factory MOQs and DTC vs grocery economics need somebody who's solved them before.

iii.

International is calling — and confusing

Distributors are pitching, foreign retailers want listings, the export grants exist. But every market has its own rules, and the wrong first move overseas can sink a UK business. I've made the mistakes so you don't have to.

iv.

You're scaling — but not professionalising

You're still the brand, the buyer, the bookkeeper, the recruiter. The team's growing but the operating model isn't. You need someone who can sit alongside you for 1–2 days a week and quietly install the rails.

The founder behind it

I've sat in your chair.

For fourteen years I co-founded and ran a premium plant-based confectionery brand – Jealous Sweets – from a London kitchen table to listings in Selfridges, Harrods, Harvey Nichols, Whole Foods, Holland & Barrett and Asda, and into eight international markets across Asia, the Middle East and beyond.

I raised angel money. I negotiated bank facilities. I won a trademark fight against a much bigger company. I picked the wrong distributor and lived with the consequences. I made the gross margin call that worked and the one that didn't. I built a team, lost a co-founder, and ultimately worked through what every consumer brand founder fears most.

That's not a CV – that's a curriculum. And it's why I now work alongside founders facing the same decisions, from the inside, not from a deck. No theories. No frameworks borrowed from McKinsey. Just hard-won, founder-grade judgement, sat next to you for as long as you need it.
