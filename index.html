Division 01 — Founder Advisory

I've built it, broken it, scaled it & sold it. Now I help you do it.

Interim and fractional support for FMCG and consumer brand founders, sub-£5m revenue. Commercial, financial, and operational expertise from someone who's actually lived the journey.

Who I Work With

For founders who've raised the money. Now they have to make it work.

The £500k–£5m revenue band is where most consumer brands either find their stride or quietly run out of road. The gap between "promising brand" and "real business" is paved with decisions founders are rarely equipped to make alone.

i.
You've landed your first big retailer
Whether it's your first major supermarket like Asda or a premium independent — getting the listing is only the start. Managing listing fees, navigating promotional cycles every six months (which catch founders off guard), knowing what marketing to do, and how to engage buyers effectively — that's where the real work begins.
ii.
Cash is tight but ambition is bigger
You've raised an angel round, perhaps a seed. The runway is real but finite. Working capital, gross margin, factory MOQs and DTC vs grocery economics need somebody who's solved them before. I had my finger on the pulse every single day — and always maintained a 12-month runway to plan for anything, including the unexpected.
iii.
International is calling — and confusing
So many foreign markets, so many pitfalls. You need to know where to prioritise and where to focus. There are red herrings everywhere. You need to know how to deal with payment terms — everyone will want credit, and that's not always the way to go. I've navigated eight markets so you don't have to repeat my mistakes.
iv.
You're scaling — but not professionalising
You're still the brand, the buyer, the bookkeeper, the recruiter. The team's growing but the operating model isn't. From the start at Jealous Sweets, I utilised my experience at PwC to help build us the right foundations. Now more than ever, using AI, you can generate processes and systems that scale your business very fast. I'll help you install the rails.

The Founder Behind It

I've sat in your chair.

For fourteen years I co-founded and ran a premium plant-based confectionery brand — Jealous Sweets — from a London kitchen table to listings in Virgin Airlines, Selfridges, Harrods, Harvey Nichols, Whole Foods, Holland & Barrett and Asda, and into eight international markets across Asia, the Middle East and beyond.

I personally raised over £1m in angel funding — every single shareholder in the business came via me. I negotiated bank facilities. I won a trademark fight against a much bigger company. I picked the wrong distributor and lived with the consequences.

I navigated the business through Brexit — our manufacturer is based in France, which created severe EUR/GBP currency exposure and supply chain disruption overnight. I steered us through COVID when the entire foodservice and travel retail sector shut down. And in early 2022, I managed the business through global commodity price shocks that saw sugar prices rise by over 40%, hammering our cost base at a time when we could least afford it.

Before all of that, my training and experience at PwC gave me deep insight into entering any business and understanding where all the risks are — assessing the cradle-to-grave scenario from start to finish, identifying where processes and systems are strong, where they are adequate, and where they need improving. That framework applies whether it's finance, FMCG, or any sector I walk into.

That's not a CV — that's a curriculum. And it's why I now work alongside founders facing the same decisions, from the inside, not from a deck. No theories. No frameworks borrowed from McKinsey. Just hard-won, founder-grade judgement, sat next to you for as long as you need it.
